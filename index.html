Control that makes electric motion quiet, efficient and safe

The Eco-friendly Mobility Control & Systems Lab designs motor drive and control systems for electric vehicles and robots. From PMSM torque control and NVH reduction to inverter-based battery heating, we build control laws from dynamics models and prove them on real hardware.

Yonghun Kim, Ph.D. 김용훈

Principal Investigator · Associate Professor, School of Mechanical Engineering

Yonghun Kim received his B.S., M.S. and Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from KAIST, where his doctoral work introduced disturbance observer based robust torque control and a novel flux-weakening method for permanent magnet synchronous motors. Before joining CNU he was a research assistant professor at KAIST's Eco-friendly Smart Vehicle Research Center.
