Research

Six questions, one method

We work on dynamic control systems for eco-friendly vehicles and robots. Whether the plant is a traction PMSM, a DC/DC converter or a two-wheeled vehicle, we start from a model, design a robust observer-based control law, and prove it on hardware. Our scope covers everything from electric motor drive control to vehicle dynamics modeling.

01 · Model

Dynamics and electrical models of the drive, converter or vehicle, with the uncertainties that matter in production hardware made explicit.

02 · Control law

Disturbance observers, pole-zero cancellation, order reduction and model-free adaptive observers that recover nominal performance under parameter drift.

03 · Hardware

DSP implementation, inverter and dynamometer tests, and vehicle or robot trials, often with industry partners in the EV and robotics sector.
